# Findings for management

Northgate Distribution (fictional), deliveries in the 90 days to 25 Oct 2026. Generated by `analytics/pipeline/findings.py`.

## Where we are

- 76.3% on time against a 90% target: 1,010 late deliveries out of 4,268.
- Lanes through Chattogram port carry 65% of deliveries and 75% of the late ones.
- Carrier choice is the biggest controllable factor. On SVR-CGP, Padma Freight Lines ran 84% on time and Karnaphuli Movers 59%.
- Road on-time drops to 71% around Eid, from 80%. The monsoon months run 8 points below the rest of the year.
- Cost per delivery: ৳59,609.

## What to change first (estimated per 90 days)

| Change | Late deliveries avoided | Extra cost |
|---|---:|---:|
| Move 30% of the weakest carrier's loads on each road lane to the best one | 47 | ৳11.6 lakh |
| Move 40% of 4–9 pm truck dispatches to earlier in the day | 13 | none |
| Pre-book Eid trucks, closing half the Eid drop (8% premium) | 12 | ৳2.8 lakh |
| **Together** | **72** | **৳14.5 lakh** |

On-time delivery would go from 76.3% to about 78.0%, at ৳14.5 lakh a quarter, or ৳20,043 per late delivery avoided. That does not reach 90%: most of the rest is port and highway congestion around Chattogram.

## Caveats

- Simulated data. The method carries over to real data; the figures do not.
- Assumes the better carrier has spare capacity and keeps its on-time rate with more volume.
- Late-delivery penalties are not in the data, so only the cost side of each change has a taka figure.
